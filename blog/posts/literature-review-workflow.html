A Lightweight Literature Review Workflow

February 12, 2026 · 10 min read

A literature review can quickly become chaotic. This workflow helps me retain key ideas and convert reading into experiments.

Step 1: Build a short queue

Keep no more than 10 papers in the active queue. Everything else stays in a backlog.

Step 2: Structured note-taking

For each paper, write: problem, method, assumptions, limits, and one possible extension. This can be done in 20 minutes.

Step 3: Weekly synthesis map

At the end of the week, group papers by method family and identify gaps. Those gaps become hypotheses for the next sprint.

Reading without synthesis is consumption. Reading with synthesis is research.

This process is intentionally lightweight so it remains sustainable during intense experimental periods.
